# Films go here (home/)

MP4 (H.264). No sound needed: films play muted. The build stops if a film is over its limit.

| File name | Length | Size (px) | Limit | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| home/film.mp4 | 10 to 20 s | 1920 × 1080 | 8 MB | Full-width film on the homepage |
| home/hero-bf.mp4 | 6 to 10 s loop | 1920 × 1200 | 4 MB | Optional. Needs the hero photo too. Plays when Bruffles opens to 70% |
| home/hero-pp.mp4 | 6 to 10 s loop | 1920 × 1200 | 4 MB | Optional. Needs the hero photo too. Plays when Passport opens to 70% (lifestyle/brand only) |

Films never play for visitors who switched on "reduce motion" or "data saver". They see the still photo instead.

Hero films start by themselves only the first time their side opens. After that they stay paused until the visitor clicks the film or presses Space.
